Pluton lets you decide what the web gets to see.

Block it before it loads.

Pluton Shield runs a local proxy and sets it as your Windows proxy. Every request is checked by the engine, then blocked, swapped for a harmless stand-in, or stripped of its tracking parameters. Leftover ad slots are hidden on the page.

Pluton Shield on GitHub

A browser that blocks by default.

NEAT carries its own blocker: adblock-rust fed by uBlock Origin lists. It stops requests and popups, hides what is left, runs scriptlets before the page’s own scripts, and gives you a switch for each site.

NEAT on GitHub

Look different to every site.

NEAT nudges what a page can read, such as canvas, audio and hardware details. Values stay steady on one site, differ on the next, and change again after a restart, so two sites can’t match their visitor.

See how it works

Sealed on your machine.

Logins, one-time codes, cards and identities are sealed with AES-256-GCM. The key is wrapped for your Windows user, and showing or copying a password asks for Windows Hello or your NEAT password.
